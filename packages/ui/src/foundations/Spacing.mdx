# Spacing

Space is one scale, `space.1` to `space.7`. Gaps, padding and margins take a step of it, so that the same distance
means the same thing everywhere: a hairline between a label and its field, a section's breath between sections.

- Space between siblings with `gap`, in a flex or grid layout, not with margins on each, which collapse or double.
- Related things sit closer together than unrelated ones: a field's label and hint `space.1`, fields `space.4`,
  sections `space.6`.

## Radii, borders and widths

          <code>{size.path}</code>
        </td>
        <td>
          <code>{size.css}</code>
        </td>
        <td>{size.description}</td>
        <td>
          <code>{cssVariable(size.path)}</code>
        </td>
      </tr>
    ))}
  </tbody>
</table>

- A border, a fill or a radius marks something as an object of its own. Use them by role, for what needs setting
  apart, not on every block.
- A page's text runs no wider than `layout.content`; a dialog no wider than `layout.dialog`.
- Every page works at a phone's width, with a side gutter of `space.4` at least, and never scrolls sideways: only a
  table or code may be wider, scrolling inside its own box.
