# Motion

Mondarth's pages move little. Motion says something changed, or where something went: a dialog opening, a section
unfolding. It does not decorate.

- **Nothing moves for a reader who prefers reduced motion.** Wrap every transition and animation in
  `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`, so that it is added for those who want it, never taken away from
  those who do not.
- Nothing flashes, and nothing moves on its own for more than five seconds without a way to stop it.
- What a page says is there at rest: nothing waits for an animation to be seen.
