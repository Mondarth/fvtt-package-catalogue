# Accessibility

Every Mondarth page is for everyone who comes to it: with a keyboard, a screen reader, a magnifier, a phone, or
in a hurry. The aim is WCAG 2.2 at AA.

## What is checked

- **Every story is checked by [axe](https://github.com/dequelabs/axe-core)**, and a violation fails its test, and so
  CI. The Accessibility panel below a story shows what axe found.
- **Every text colour reaches 4.5:1 contrast** on every background, and the focus ring and controls' edges 3:1, in
  both themes: the design tokens' tests check it.
- The applications' end-to-end tests find their way by role and name, as a screen reader does, so a control with no
  name fails them.

axe finds what can be found by looking at the markup. The rest is for whoever writes the component to check.

## What every component and page does

- **Semantic HTML first**: a `<button>` for an action, a link for a place, a `<label>` for every field, a heading for
  every section, in order. ARIA only for what HTML cannot say.
- **Everything works by keyboard**, in a sensible order, with the focus always visible (`color.focus`,
  `border.width.focus`). Nothing traps the focus but a dialog, which gives it back on closing.
- **Every image has a text alternative**, or an empty `alt` where the words beside it say it already, as the logo
  beside a page's name.
- **Nothing is said by colour alone**: a status has words; a link in text is underlined.
- **Problems are announced**: a callout is an alert and takes the focus; a field it is about names it with
  `aria-describedby`.
- **A disabled control says why** it is disabled, beside it, named by `aria-describedby`.
- **Text can grow** to 200% without anything being cut off, and pages reflow at a phone's width without scrolling
  sideways.
- **Motion respects the reader**: see [Motion](?path=/docs/foundations-motion--docs).
- **Language**: each page says its language (`lang`), and any words in another say theirs, as the language picker
  names each language in itself.
